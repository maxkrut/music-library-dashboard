# Genre review — 2026-09-23

The local audit scanned all **2,020 tracks and 1,401 distinct credited artist names**. Targeted source checks and curated rules changed genre labels on **139 tracks across 95 lead artists**, including **129 primary-genre changes**. Three further recordings have unverified labels withheld from statistics. **2,017 tracks have usable primary genres**, all mapped to named genre families. This measures coverage, not independent verification of every recording.

The dashboard groups recordings by the **lead artist's most frequent primary genre in this library**. One atypical release does not create a second category for the band; its release-specific genre remains in the track data. Six tied artists and seven career-wide exceptions use documented choices in [artist_genre_profiles.csv](data/artist_genre_profiles.csv); unreviewed ties use the library-wide genre frequency and then alphabetical order. Ten further profiles confirm genres already chosen by the vote. These confirmations never override a future vote; the audit flags any later disagreement. Ministry keeps synth-pop on its *With Sympathy* tracks while appearing as industrial metal in the artist atlas. Enslaved and Borknagar appear as progressive black metal, Aura Noir as black thrash metal, and Dark Suns as progressive metal. Emma Ruth Rundle appears as singer-songwriter despite two saved collaborations with Thou, while The Gathering appears as atmospheric rock rather than the gothic metal of its early albums. Individual recordings keep their own primary labels. Spotify artist IDs keep namesakes apart, while two Amenra profiles are joined as one group. Guest artists do not determine the track's category. The genre audit below still checks the recording-level labels that feed these artist votes.

The review now uses **177 Spotify-ID rules**: 127 correction rules, 41 recording-specific confirmations, three explicit review holds, and six legacy defaults narrowed to the lead artist's ID. Rules distinguish namesakes, preserve collaborations, and keep album-specific exceptions ahead of artist defaults. Evidence URLs are recorded in [genre_rules.csv](data/genre_rules.csv). Confirmation notes distinguish an independently supported primary style from a broad family check; existing secondary tags are not all independently verified.

## Remaining uncertainty

Rule coverage is now explicit: **315 recordings match source-linked rules**, **294 match legacy rules without recorded sources**, **1,408 have no matching local rule**, and **three are withheld**. The first group includes broad artist-level confirmations and inferred classifications documented in rule notes; source-linked does not mean that each recording or secondary tag was independently verified. The latter two groups passed the automated checks but were not all independently checked against release sources. A clean automated queue therefore does not certify the whole library. The private report keeps these counts separately from confirmed errors and review holds.

The detailed private report keeps **72 artist names** with diagnostic flags: 71 cache-name mismatches and one repeated Spotify identity; three of these artists also have an explicitly unverified genre. The cache-name mismatches include transliterations, aliases, and corrected recordings whose old cache remains. They are separate from unresolved recording genres and are not 72 confirmed genre errors. A further 113 names occur only as guests and cannot be assigned a reliable solo style from these recordings.

**Three recordings remain explicitly unverified.** Their raw labels are preserved with `genre_status=unverified`, but do not contribute to genre counts, Spotify fallback tags, or artist genre summaries.
